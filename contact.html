Store Policies & Warranty

🚚 Shipping & Delivery Policy

We dispatch orders through leading courier services (TCS & Leopards Courier) across all cities in Pakistan. Orders are processed within 24 hours of placement. Standard transit time is 2-4 working days. Delivery charges are Rs. 250, with FREE delivery on all orders of Rs. 5,000 and above.

🛡️ 7-Day Checking Warranty & Return Policy

Customer satisfaction is our highest priority. All timepieces include a 7-day checking warranty covering manufacturing defects or movement irregularity. If you encounter any transit damage or issue upon delivery, message us on WhatsApp with your Order ID and parcel photo for an immediate replacement.

🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

We strictly respect your privacy. Customer contact details, addresses, and telephone numbers are used exclusively for dispatching and courier tracking. We do not sell or share customer data with third parties, and we never request or store sensitive banking passwords or payment card credentials.
